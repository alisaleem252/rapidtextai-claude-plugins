# SEO audit checklist

Weights add up to 100. A pass earns the full weight, a warn earns half, a fail earns nothing. Leave "not checked" items out of the total and scale the score to 100.

## Core (60)

| Check | Weight | Pass | Warn | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Title tag | 10 | 30–60 characters, keyword near the start, unique wording | Too long or too short, or keyword late | Missing, or the site name only |
| Meta description | 6 | 70–160 characters, keyword included, gives a reason to click | Too long or too short | Missing or duplicated from the title |
| One H1 matching intent | 8 | Exactly one H1 containing the keyword or a close variant | H1 present but off-topic, or more than one | No H1 |
| Heading structure | 5 | H2s and H3s in order, covering the subtopics | Skipped levels, or headings used for styling | No subheadings on a long page |
| Content depth | 10 | Answers the query fully for its type (often 800+ words for an article) | Thin for the query, padded, or off-topic sections | Under 300 words for an informational query |
| Keyword use | 6 | Keyword and natural variants in the first 100 words and in headings | Present but only once, or stuffed | Absent |
| Internal links | 5 | 3+ descriptive links to related pages on the same site | 1–2 links, or "click here" anchors | None |
| Freshness | 5 | Current year or a recent "updated" date, no stale facts | Undated | Old years, prices, versions or "latest" claims that are now wrong |
| Search intent | 5 | Format matches what ranks (guide, list, product, tool) | Partly matches | Wrong format for the query |

## Technical (25)

| Check | Weight | Pass | Warn | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Indexable | 6 | No `noindex`, page returns content | — | `noindex` meta or header |
| Canonical | 4 | Self-referencing or correct canonical | Missing | Points to another page by mistake |
| URL | 3 | Short, lowercase, hyphenated, readable | Long or with parameters | IDs only |
| Images | 4 | Every content image has descriptive alt text | Some alt text missing | No alt text |
| Structured data | 4 | Relevant schema (Article, Product, FAQ, Breadcrumb) | Present but incomplete | None on a page type that benefits |
| Mobile and Open Graph | 4 | Viewport meta, `og:title`, `og:image` | Some missing | No viewport meta |

## Trust (15)

| Check | Weight | Pass | Warn | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Author and expertise | 5 | Named author with a bio or credentials | Author name only | Anonymous on a YMYL topic |
| Sources | 5 | Stats and claims link to credible sources | Some claims unsourced | Many specific claims, no sources |
| External links | 2 | Links out to authoritative pages where useful | — | None on a research-heavy page |
| Readability | 3 | Short paragraphs, plain language, lists where useful | Long walls of text in places | Hard to read throughout |
